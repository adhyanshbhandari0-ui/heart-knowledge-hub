EFO is a data-driven ontology that imports parts of existing community ontologies into a single framework. It is used for annotation, curation, query and visualisation of data by the Gene Expression Atlas, NHGRI GWAS Catalog, BioSamples database, and is being mapped to Ensembl variation.
