# BioGRID

**Source:** https://thebiogrid.org

---

Welcome to our Database of Protein, Genetic and Chemical Interactions

BioGRID is a biomedical interaction repository with data compiled through comprehensive curation efforts. 
                     Our current index is version 5.0.263 and searches 88,939 publications 
                     for 3,050,286 protein
                     and genetic interactions, 31,540 chemical interactions and 1,128,339 post translational modifications
                     from major model organism species. All data are freely provided via our search index and available for download in many standardized formats.

The BioGRID Open Respository of CRISPR Screens (ORCS) is a publicly accessible database of CRISPR screens compiled through comprehensive curation of all genome-wide CRISPR screen data reported in the biomedical literature. ORCS is updated on a quarterly basis and is fully searchable by gene/protein, phenotype, cell line, authors, and other attributes. Each screen recorded in ORCS is accompanied by structured metadata annotation that captures salient CRISPR experimental details. All data in ORCS can be downloaded in standard formats.

BioGRID themed curation projects focus on specific biological processes with disease relevance. Core genes/proteins central to the process are assembled with expert input and relevant publications curated for biological interactions. Themed curation projects are updated monthly and additional projects are generated on a regular basis. See current projects on Parkinson's Disease , Cell Cycle - Senescence , Cell Cycle - mTOR pathway , Cell Cycle - Mitotic Spindle Assembly , Arachidonic Acid Pathway , Synthetic Protein Interaction Project , Human Papilloma Virus (HPV) , Autism spectrum disorder , Alzheimer's Disease , COVID-19 Coronavirus , Fanconi Anemia , Glioblastoma , the Yeast Kinome , the Ubiquitin-Proteasome System and Autophagy .
