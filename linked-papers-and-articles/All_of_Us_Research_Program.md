# All of Us Research Program

**Source:** https://www.researchallofus.org

---

The National Institutes of Health’s All of Us Research Program is building one of the largest biomedical data resources of its kind. The All of Us Research Hub stores health data from participants from across the United States.

Register for the Researcher Workbench to access data and tools to conduct health research and improve understanding of health and disease.

Data Snapshots showcase the breadth and depth of the All of Us Research Program dataset. The snapshots provide participant demographics, geographic distribution, and more. We update the snapshots daily.

The Researcher Workbench is a secure cloud-based platform used for data analysis and collaboration. Registered researchers can access individual-level data, including genomic data, along with a suite of tools and resources to support research projects.

The Researcher Workbench is a growing data ecosystem that brings together an expanding portfolio of research datasets, including the Environmental influences on Child Health Outcomes ( ECHO ) Program data as a major recent addition.

The Data Browser provides interactive views of aggregate-level participant data in the All of Us dataset. Explore the types and quantities of data available or plan a project before registering for the Researcher Workbench.

Registered researchers provide descriptions of their Researcher Workbench projects so that other researchers, participants, and the public can learn how the dataset is being used.
