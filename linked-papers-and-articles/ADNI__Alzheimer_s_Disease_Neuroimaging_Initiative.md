Sharing Alzheimer’s research data with the world

The Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) is a longitudinal, multi-center, observational study.

The overall goal of ADNI is to validate biomarkers for Alzheimer’s disease (AD) clinical trials.

See the impact that ADNI has had on ADRD Research

This tool provides interactive views of aggregate-level clinical data contributed longitudinally by over 2,500 participants around the U.S. and Canada.
