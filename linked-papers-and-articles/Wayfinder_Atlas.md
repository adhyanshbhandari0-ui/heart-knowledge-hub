Wayfinder Atlas tracks 61 jurisdictions, verified October 2026. A settled, lawful medical pathway exists in 2 for psilocybin therapy, 2 for MDMA-assisted therapy, and 35 for ketamine or esketamine therapy. Ketamine is available off-label far more widely; psilocybin and MDMA therapy remain investigational almost everywhere else.

Switzerland and Australia offer a settled, lawful psilocybin-therapy pathway as of October 2026. Others that Wayfinder flags as its own reading of unsettled law include United States. Each country page links the dated primary source.

Switzerland and Australia provide a settled, lawful MDMA-assisted-therapy pathway through supervised medical programs. In Liechtenstein, Canada, New Zealand and Israel, MDMA is available outside trials, if at all, only through named-patient or compassionate-use approvals. Everywhere else MDMA therapy remains investigational, reachable only through clinical trials.

Ketamine itself is a licensed anaesthetic, prescribed off-label for depression in many countries. Esketamine (Spravato) is an approved depression medicine with a settled, reimbursed pathway in 35 of the 61 jurisdictions tracked. That is different from psilocybin or MDMA therapy, which stay investigational in most of the world.

Wayfinder Atlas tracks 37 European jurisdictions. Esketamine therapy is reimbursed across much of the continent, but a settled psilocybin or MDMA therapy pathway is still rare in Europe. The map and table show each country's status against its own dated primary sources.
