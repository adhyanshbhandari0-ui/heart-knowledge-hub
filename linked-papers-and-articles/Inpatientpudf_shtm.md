Texas Inpatient Public Use Data File (PUDF)

The Texas Inpatient Public Use Data File (IP PUDF) contains data on discharges from Texas hospitals.

The files contain anonymized and de-identified data to protect the privacy of patients. Personal identifiers, such as names and addresses, are removed. However, the file still holds valuable information for research and other purposes.

PUDF data collected from 2021– 2026 must be requested via the Inpatient PUDF Order Form and may impose a fee. Data collected from 2005 – 2019 is available for free download from the Free IP PUDF page .

Complete the IP PUDF Order Form , which includes a Data Use Agreement (DUA). Review the form for further instructions and submission guidelines.

Under Texas Health and Safety Code Section 108.012(b) , the Texas Department of State Health Services (DSHS) may charge a fee for public use or provider quality data.

Fees are set to cover the program’s operational costs. Fees depend on how much data is requested, the cost of preparing it. No fee is charged when data is requested by another Texas state agency. Texas state agencies and universities, as well as local Texas health departments, are exempt from paying for fees.

For a detailed breakdown of fees, see Data Fees .
