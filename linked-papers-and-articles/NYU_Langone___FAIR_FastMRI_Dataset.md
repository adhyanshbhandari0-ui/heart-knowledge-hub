# NYU Langone & FAIR FastMRI Dataset

**Source:** https://registry.opendata.aws/nyu-fastmri

---

biology health image processing imaging life sciences magnetic resonance imaging neurobiology neuroimaging

This dataset contains deidentified raw k-space data and DICOM image files of over 1,500 knees and 6,970 brains.

The dataset is estimated to grow annually to include MRI raw data and imaging for additional body structures.
