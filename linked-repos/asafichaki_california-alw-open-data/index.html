California Assisted Living Waiver Open Data

County-level participating-facility and licensed-capacity data for California's Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver, with statewide enrollment and waitlist totals from the California Department of Health Care Services.

14,847 enrolled18,365 waiting15 participating counties

Important limitation: DHCS reports one statewide waitlist. This dataset does not estimate county-level waitlists.

Sources and context

Facility counts and licensed capacity are derived from the DHCS participating-facilities GIS dataset. Enrollment and waitlist totals come from the DHCS enrollment and waitlist report.

See the human-readable tracker and county breakdowns at California Care Compass, or inspect the source repository on GitHub.
