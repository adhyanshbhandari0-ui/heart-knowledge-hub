Verified Supplement Evidence Database

An open, evidence-graded dataset of dietary-supplement clinical data — dosing, form/bioavailability, drug–nutrient interactions, population need, and cost-per-effective-dose product comparisons. Every clinical claim carries a PubMed PMID.
